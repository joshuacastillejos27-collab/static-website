About This Site

Wander Well was built as a resource for students and new travelers planning their first big trips abroad. Whether you're looking for destination inspiration or just want to track a countdown to your next departure, this site brings a few handy tools together in one place.

Why travel?

  • Experience new cultures and cuisines firsthand
  • Build confidence navigating unfamiliar places
  • Create memories that last far longer than souvenirs
  • Meet people you'd never otherwise cross paths with
