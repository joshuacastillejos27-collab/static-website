Our Story

Built by Travelers, for Travelers

Compass & Cloud started as a class project and grew into a small resource for students and first-time travelers planning their first big trip abroad. It brings destination inspiration and a few handy planning tools together in one place.

Why travel?

  • - Experience new cultures and cuisines firsthand
  • - Build confidence navigating unfamiliar places
  • - Create memories that last far longer than souvenirs
  • - Meet people you'd never otherwise cross paths with
